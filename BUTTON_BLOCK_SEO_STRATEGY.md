## 🎯 CRITICAL FINDINGS FROM BUTTON BLOCK BLOGS

### **1. The AI Search Revolution (2026 Reality)**
- **60%+ of searches** now end without a click (zero-click)
- **25% of organic traffic** will shift to AI chatbots by 2026 (Gartner)
- **AI-referred sessions up 527%** (Jan-May 2025)
- **Fort Wayne advantage**: Lower competition than major metros

### **2. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) - The New SEO**
Traditional SEO vs AEO:
| Aspect | Traditional SEO | AEO |
|--------|----------------|-----|
| Goal | Rank in blue links | Be THE answer AI provides |
| Content | Keyword-optimized | Q&A structured, entity-rich |
| Success | Click-through rate | Citation rate in AI responses |
| Timeline | 6-12 months | 2-4 months for AI visibility |

**Key Insight**: Pages with FAQ schema are **3.2x more likely** to appear in Google AI Overviews.

### **3. The 40-60 Word Rule**
Every section must start with a **40-60 word direct answer** that AI can extract:
- Sentence 1: Direct answer (yes/no or core fact)
- Sentences 2-3: Supporting explanation
- Self-contained (no "as mentioned above")

### **4. FAQ Schema is CRITICAL**
- Only **12.4%** of websites implement structured data
- Websites with schema get **4x more rich snippets**
- FAQ schema works for AI even if rich results are restricted
- Must match visible content exactly

### **5. Google Business Profile (GBP) is #1 Asset**
- Appears in Local Pack, Google Maps, AI Overviews
- Many searchers never visit your website
- Must be 100% complete with photos, posts, reviews

### **6. Fort Wayne Market Specifics**
- **270,000+ residents** in Fort Wayne
- **400,000+ in metro area**
- Mobile search: **65%+** of local searches
- Voice search: **20%** of local queries
- Local searchers use "Fort Wayne" not just "near me"

## 🚀 IMPLEMENTATION PRIORITIES

### **PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Week 1)**

#### 1.1 FAQ Schema Implementation
**Every page needs FAQ schema:**
- Homepage
- About page
- Floor plans page
- Each location page
- Each guide page
- Each model detail page

**Structure:**
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "What is the 40-60 word question?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "40-60 word direct answer. First sentence is the core answer. Supporting details follow."
      }
    }
  ]
}
```

#### 1.2 LocalBusiness Schema
**Complete schema with:**
- Name, Address, Phone (NAP)
- Geo-coordinates
- Service area (all 6 states)
- Hours
- Services offered
- Images
- Reviews

#### 1.3 Service Schema
**For each service:**
- Manufactured homes
- Modular homes
- Delivery
- Setup
- Site work

#### 1.4 HowTo Schema
**For guides:**
- How to buy a manufactured home
- How to prepare your land
- How to finance
