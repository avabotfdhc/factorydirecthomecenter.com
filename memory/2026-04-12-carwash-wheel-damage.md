## Research Summary: Car Wash Wheel Damage Complaints

### Common Types of Car Wash Wheel Damage

Based on industry knowledge and consumer reports, automatic car washes (including tube/tunnel car washes) cause several types of wheel damage:

**1. Tire Shine Applicator Damage**
- The tire shine machine uses spinning applicators (often brushes or pads) that contact the tire sidewall
- If misaligned, these can contact the wheel rim directly, causing:
  - Scratches and scuff marks on alloy wheels
  - Paint chipping on coated wheels
  - Curb-rash-style damage on the outer rim lip
  - Damage to custom or low-profile wheels

**2. Guide Rail Damage**
- The guide rails that keep your car centered can scrape wheels if:
  - The driver doesn't align properly
  - The rails are misaligned
  - Low-profile or wide wheels extend beyond the rail clearance

**3. Track/Dolly Damage**
- Some conveyor systems use dollies that grip tires
- Improper positioning can cause sidewall damage or wheel contact

**4. Brush Contact**
- While less common with modern "touchless" systems, some tunnel washes still use brushes that can contact wheels

### Why Car Washes Deny Claims

Car washes typically deny damage claims using these arguments:

1. **"Our equipment can't damage wheels"** — The regional manager's claim is standard deflection
2. **Pre-existing damage** — They'll claim the damage was already there
3. **User error** — You didn't align properly or followed instructions incorrectly
4. **Signage disclaimers** — "Not responsible for damage" signs (often unenforceable)
5. **Lack of proof** — You can't prove it happened there

### Legal Reality

- **Bailment Law**: When you hand your car to a business for service, they have a duty of care
- **Disclaimers are often unenforceable** — Signs saying "not responsible" don't absolve negligence
- **Small claims court** is your ultimate recourse if they refuse

- Take clear, well-lit photos of both damaged rims from multiple angles
