## 2. REGULATIONS & POLICY CHANGES

### ROAD to Housing Act 2025
- Bipartisan legislation championed by Rep. Emanuel Cleaver (D-MO)
- MHARR calling for amendments or removal from NDAA
- Controversy over "removable chassis" provisions

### DOE Energy Regulations
- MHARR urging President Trump to withdraw/repeal
- Industry concern about cost increases

### HUD Leadership
- Secretary Scott Turner confirmed
- Active deregulation focus ("tear down antiquated pandemic-era regulations")
- Strong industry engagement

### Zoning & Land-Use
- Persistent NIMBYism blocking developments
- Resident evictions rising as parks sold
- Delaware considering resident protection laws

## 3. COMPETITOR ACTIVITIES & PRICING

### Industry Consolidation Concerns
- "Buffett-style moats" harming affordability
- Major players: Clayton Homes, 21st Mortgage, Champion Homes, Cavco (CVCO), ELS, Sun Communities
- MHI opposed to resident-owned communities
- Price rigging/antitrust concerns in some markets

### Terminology Shift
- Industry pushing "attainable" vs "affordable" housing
- Signals premium positioning strategy

## 4. CONSUMER DEMAND & BUYING PATTERNS

### Demand Drivers
- **7.1 million affordable home shortage** (NLIHC Gap 2025)
- Rising "forever renters" and homelessness
- Manufactured homes recognized as "unsubsidized affordable housing"
- Homes appreciate similarly to conventional housing (Furman Center)

### Consumer Sentiment
- Manufactured home residents = swing state voting bloc
- Housing affordability ranks high among voter concerns
- Fannie Mae providing financing guidance
