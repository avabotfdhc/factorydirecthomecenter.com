## Documents You'll Need

Based on the BMV packet, you'll typically need:

1. **Affidavit of Sale or Disposal** (State Form 50635)
2. **Proof of land ownership** — Your Sheriff's Deed
3. **Proof the home is abandoned** — Documentation showing:
   - Foreclosure/sheriff sale records
   - Previous owner has been gone 60+ days
   - No one has claimed the home
4. **Photos of the home** — Exterior showing condition
5. **VIN/Serial number if available** — If not, the BMV may assign one
6. **Affidavit stating you tried to find the owner** — Certified mail attempts, etc.
7. **Application fee** — Check with BMV for current amount

## Process

1. **Download the packet:** https://www.in.gov/bmv/files/Abandoned_Mobile_Home_Packet.pdf
2. **Complete all forms**
3. **Submit to BMV Central Office:**
   - **Address:** Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles, Central Office, 100 N. Senate Ave., Indianapolis, IN 46204
   - **Phone:** (317) 233-6000
4. **Wait for processing** — They'll research and either issue title or request more info

## Important Notes

- This process is for **private landowners** — you qualify
- The BMV will attempt to notify the last registered owner
- If no one contests, you get a clean title
- If the serial number is truly gone, they may assign a new one
