- Fort Wayne Metals (founded 1970)

## 🏛️ HISTORICAL NOTES

### The Teders Name
The surname "Teders" is believed to be an altered form of "Teter," of German origin. In 1880, approximately 61% of all recorded Teders families in the USA resided in Indiana.

### The Desmonds Name
The surname "Desmond" is of Irish origin, an Anglicized form of Gaelic "Ó Deasmhumhnaigh," meaning "descendant of the man from southern Munster."

### Avilla, Indiana
A small town in Noble County, Indiana, where the Teders family established deep roots. Multiple generations attended Avilla High School.
