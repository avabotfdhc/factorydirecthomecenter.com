# Emergency Housing Campaign: Michigan Tornado Recovery

**Campaign Name:** "Rebuild Fast, Rebuild Strong"  
**Target Areas:** Union City, Three Rivers, Edwardsburg, Michigan  
**Event:** EF-3/EF-2 Tornadoes - March 6-7, 2026  
**Campaign Type:** Emergency Response / Disaster Recovery

---

## Situation Overview

**Union City, MI (Branch County)**
- EF-3 tornado (165 mph winds, 1 mph shy of EF-4)
- 3 fatalities, 12 injured
- Severe damage around Union Lake and downtown
- 2,200+ without power
- State of emergency declared

**Three Rivers, MI (St. Joseph County)**
- EF-2 tornado (130+ mph winds)
- Destroyed homes, blown-out windows
- Menards store roof ripped off
- Multiple structures damaged

**Edwardsburg, MI (Cass County)**
- Significant tornado damage
- 1 fatality (12-year-old), multiple injuries
- Homes and pole barns destroyed
- 500+ without power
- Cass County state of emergency

## Messaging Guidelines

### DO:
- Lead with empathy and help
- Emphasize speed and quality
- Mention insurance/FEMA assistance
- Offer free consultations
- Be specific about timeline (4-6 weeks)

### DON'T:
- Be pushy or salesy
- Use fear tactics
- Make promises about insurance coverage
- Disparage traditional construction
- Ignore the emotional impact

---

## Compliance & Sensitivity Notes

1. **Avoid predatory messaging** — This is help, not opportunism
2. **Be accurate about timelines** — Don't promise what you can't deliver
3. **Respect privacy** — Don't use specific addresses of damaged homes
4. **Insurance clarity** — Don't make claims about what insurance will cover
5. **FEMA coordination** — Be helpful but don't position as official FEMA partner unless authorized
