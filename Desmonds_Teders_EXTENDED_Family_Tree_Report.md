## 🏛️ HISTORICAL NOTES

### German Immigration to Indiana:
German immigrants were the largest ethnic heritage group among white Hoosiers for much of Indiana's history. Many settled in southwestern Indiana and cities like Indianapolis (known as "Germantown"). The Teders family was part of this wave, with 61% of all Teders families in the USA residing in Indiana by 1880.

### Irish Immigration to Fort Wayne:
Many Irish immigrants arrived in Fort Wayne in the mid-19th century to work on the Wabash & Erie Canal and later the railroads. St. Patrick's Church was founded around 1889 to minister to Irish families. The Desmonds name (Ó Deasmhumhnaigh) originates from County Cork, Ireland.

### Fort Wayne's Industrial Heritage:
Fort Wayne was known as "The Magnet Wire Capital of the World" due to companies like Dudlo Manufacturing (1914), Rea Magnet Wire (1933), and Essex Co. Sherman Desmonds worked at Indiana Rod & Wire during this industrial heyday.
