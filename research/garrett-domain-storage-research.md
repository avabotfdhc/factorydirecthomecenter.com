### The Challenge

### Option 1: Contact via GoDaddy's Domain Owner Contact Form
GoDaddy provides a way to contact private domain owners:
- **For garrettcampersales.com:** https://www.godaddy.com/whois/results.aspx?domain=garrettcampersales.com&action=contactDomainOwner
- **For garrettcamperstorage.com:** https://www.godaddy.com/whois/results.aspx?domain=garrettcamperstorage.com&action=contactDomainOwner

This sends a message through GoDaddy's system to the owner without revealing their identity.

### Option 2: Direct Outreach (If You Know the Owner)
If you know who "Garrett" is in your local area (Garrett, Indiana?), you could reach out directly. The fact that:
- garrettcampersales.com was registered in 2000 (very early)
- garrettcamperstorage.com was registered in 2025 (recent)
- Both use the same privacy service

Suggests the **same person/entity owns both**.

### Option 3: Wait for Expiration (Risky)
- garrettcampersales.com expires **December 27, 2026** (9 months away)
- If not renewed, it enters a grace period, then auction
- **Risk:** Owner will likely auto-renew, or someone else grabs it

### Option 4: Domain Broker Service
GoDaddy and other registrars offer domain broker services ($50-$100 fee + commission) where they negotiate on your behalf anonymously.

### Option 5: Legal/Trademark Route (If Applicable)
If you have a trademark on "Garrett Camper" or can demonstrate legitimate business interest, you could file a UDRP complaint — but this is expensive ($1,500+) and only works if the current owner is cybersquatting.
