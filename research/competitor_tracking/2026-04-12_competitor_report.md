## 5. Strategic Observations

### Threats

1. **Price Competition:** Champion Homes Center's 20% clearance discounts are aggressive and time-bound. If FDHC competes on price, margin compression likely.

2. **Geographic Overlap:** Champion ships to identical states (IN, IL, MI, OH). Direct competitor for same customer base.

3. **Factory Tour Advantage:** Champion offers 6-day factory tours - experiential marketing that builds trust and urgency.

4. **Brand Recognition:** Champion Homes manufacturer has 70+ year history and national TV exposure. Clayton has Berkshire Hathaway backing.

### Opportunities

1. **Financing Differentiation:** Champion Homes Center's financing page is minimal (just a lead form). FDHC can differentiate with detailed financing education, lender comparisons, and pre-qualification tools.

2. **Premium Positioning:** Champion focuses on entry-level ($40K-$100K). Opportunity for FDHC in $100K-$200K+ range with higher-end finishes and multi-section homes.

3. **Content Marketing Gap:** Neither competitor has robust educational content about the buying process, zoning, permitting. FDHC can own this space.

4. **Local Presence:** Champion operates from Topeka, IN. FDHC can emphasize local Indiana presence and faster response times.

5. **Review Generation:** Champion has recent reviews from Michigan customers. FDHC should prioritize review collection from satisfied customers.

### Recommended Actions

1. **Monitor Champion's April 20 sale deadline** - expect either extension or new promotion launch
2. **Develop financing comparison content** to differentiate from Champion's minimal approach
3. **Consider factory tour or model home experience** to match Champion's experiential marketing
4. **Target Michigan customers specifically** - Champion is actively serving this market
5. **Review and optimize pricing** on comparable models to Champion's clearance lineup
