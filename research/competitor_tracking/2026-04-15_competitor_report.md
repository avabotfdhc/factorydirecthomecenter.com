## 5. Strategic Observations

### Threats

1. **Aggressive Pricing from Champion Homes Center**
   - 20%+ discounts create price pressure
   - Similar shipping territory (IN/OH/MI overlap)
   - Factory direct model undercuts traditional retail

2. **Champion Brand Recognition**
   - National TV placement on Lifetime
   - "3M+ homes built" credibility marker
   - 70+ year heritage (founded 1953)

3. **Clayton's Digital Infrastructure**
   - Customer portal (MyHome Account)
   - Multi-platform social presence
   - Strong SEO for "manufactured homes near me"

### Opportunities

1. **Urgency Window**
   - Competitor sale ends April 20 — potential for FDHC to capture post-sale shoppers
   - Customers who miss the deadline may shop alternatives

2. **Financing Differentiation**
   - Champion Homes Center does not offer direct financing
   - FDHC can emphasize financing partnerships as advantage
   - Land-home package expertise is differentiator

3. **Local Presence**
   - Champion Homes Center is Topeka, IN based (same general region)
   - FDHC can emphasize local service, setup, and community relationships
   - Face-to-face customer service vs. factory-direct model

4. **Content Gaps**
   - Limited blog/educational content on competitor sites
   - Opportunity for FDHC to own SEO on "how to buy" topics
   - Video content opportunity (tours, testimonials)

### Recommendations

1. **Monitor competitor sale expiration** — prepare follow-up campaigns for April 21+
2. **Benchmark pricing** — ensure FDHC pricing is competitive post-sale
3. **Emphasize full-service advantage** — setup, delivery, skirting, inspection coordination
4. **Develop urgency campaigns** — limited-time promotions with clear expiration dates
5. **Consider virtual tour investment** — Champion has this on every model page
