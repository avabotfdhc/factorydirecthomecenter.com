## 5. STRATEGIC OBSERVATIONS

### Threats

1. **Price Pressure:** Both Champion and Factory Expo are aggressively discounting 20-30% - this sets market expectations.

2. **Brand Confusion Risk:** Champion Homes Center (Topeka, IN) has similar naming to Factory Direct Homes Center - potential customer confusion.

3. **Factory Tour Advantage:** Both major competitors offer free factory tours - experiential selling that FDHC should consider matching.

4. **Geographic Overlap:** All competitors serve Indiana, Ohio, and Michigan - direct market competition.

### Opportunities

1. **Pricing Transparency Gap:** While competitors show "Was/Now" pricing, there's opportunity for FDHC to be MORE transparent (total cost including delivery, setup, etc.)

2. **Financing Differentiation:** All competitors use third-party lenders - FDHC could partner with preferred lenders for better rates/terms.

3. **Content Marketing:** Home Nation is actively blogging - SEO opportunity for FDHC to dominate local search.

4. **Countdown Urgency:** Both competitors using April 20, 2026 deadline - FDHC could run competing promotion.

### Recommended Actions

1. **Immediate:** Consider matching or beating the $40,900 entry-level price point
2. **Short-term:** Implement countdown timer and clearance sale section on FDHC website
3. **Medium-term:** Develop factory tour program or virtual tour alternative
4. **Ongoing:** Monitor competitor pricing weekly - they appear to update regularly
