## 5. STRATEGIC OBSERVATIONS

### Threats
1. **Champion Homes Center** aggressive clearance pricing on 2023-2025 models could pressure margins
2. **Clayton's** digital tools (MyHome Designer) create higher customer engagement
3. **Cavco's** ADU focus taps into emerging housing trend (backyard cottages, multi-generational living)

### Opportunities
1. **Clearance/Overstock** positioning is working for Champion - FDHC could emphasize similar factory-direct value
2. **Virtual tours** (Matterport) are becoming standard - investment in 3D tours recommended
3. **ADU market** is growing - consider adding ADU-focused content and models
4. **Price transparency** varies by competitor - opportunity to differentiate with clearer online pricing

### Market Patterns
- **Pricing tiers** are well-established: Entry ($80K-$100K), Mid ($120K-$180K), Premium ($200K+)
- **Financing emphasis** is universal - all competitors promote lending options
- **Factory-direct messaging** is Champion's differentiator
- **Digital customization tools** are Clayton's strength

### Recommendations for FDHC
1. Monitor Champion's clearance inventory levels - may indicate production slowdown or overstock situation
2. Evaluate MyHome Designer-style tool for FDHC website
3. Consider ADU content strategy - blog posts, dedicated page, model highlights
4. Review pricing display strategy - balance transparency with lead generation needs
5. Benchmark virtual tour technology - Matterport integration appears effective
