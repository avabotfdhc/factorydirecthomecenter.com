### HUD Code Updates (Major Regulatory News)

**Historic Updates Took Effect September 15, 2025**
- 4th and 5th Sets of MHCSS updates represent most extensive changes in 30+ years
- 74 updates to reference standards
- 16 new standards
- 3 regulatory text changes
- Fire sprinkler system standards now in effect (as of September 15, 2025)

**New HUD Final Rule (2026)**
- Permits up to four homes per structure
- Introduces new multi-story designs
- Allows improved materials and layouts
- Reduces red tape for popular existing designs
- First significant shift since HUD Code adoption
- Makes HUD Code units more competitive in suburban/urban markets

## 4. Regulatory & Policy Landscape

### The ROAD to Housing Act - GAME CHANGER

**Status:** Senate passed March 12, 2026; House version (H.R.6644) reported January 15, 2026

**Key Provision - Chassis Removal:**
- Removes permanent steel chassis requirement for HUD Code manufactured homes
- Would reduce costs by $5,000-$10,000 per home (9% of single-section home cost)
- Enables more flexible floorplans and architectural designs
- Makes two-story manufactured homes more feasible
- Allows better integration into existing single-family neighborhoods
- Steel chassis could be reused for multiple units

**Why It Matters:**
- Only 5-7% of manufactured homes are ever moved after installation
- Chassis requirement dates to 1976 and has outlived original purpose
- Would expand placement options to cities and suburbs (not just rural areas)
- Could enable multilevel units, basements, and ground-level main floors
- State-by-state voluntary opt-in mechanism proposed

**Industry Response:**
- Modular Home Builders Association (MHBA) opposes removal for second-story homes citing safety concerns
- NAHB urges alignment between federal and state regulations if chassis requirement removed
- Strong bipartisan support indicates likely passage

### White House Housing Mandate
- White House has mandated housing review (specifics developing)
- Policy focus on increasing affordable housing supply
- Manufactured housing positioned as key solution

## 5. Key Insights & Opportunities

### Immediate Opportunities

1. **Chassis Removal Preparation**
   - If ROAD Act passes, dealers should prepare for new product lines
   - Opportunity to offer both chassis-on and chassis-off options
   - Educational content needed to explain difference to consumers
   - Potential for cost savings to be passed to buyers or improve margins

2. **Land-Home Package Emphasis**
   - Data clearly shows land ownership drives appreciation
   - Manufactured homes with land appreciated 70% (2019-2026) vs. 50%+ without
   - Marketing should emphasize land ownership benefits
   - Financing partnerships for land-home packages critical

3. **Affordability Messaging**
   - $141,450 median vs. $410,000 traditional creates compelling narrative
   - $678/month vs. $1,900+ monthly cost differential
   - Target first-time buyers priced out of traditional market
   - Address misconceptions about value retention with data
