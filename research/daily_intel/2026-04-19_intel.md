## 1. Policy & Regulatory News

### HUD Manufactured Housing Consensus Committee Appointments
**Date:** February 17, 2026  
**Source:** HUD.gov (Press Release HUD-26-014)

HUD announced six new appointments to the Manufactured Housing Consensus Committee (MHCC), maintaining the committee at its statutory 21-member limit. This is significant for the industry as the MHCC advises HUD on revisions to the Manufactured Home Construction and Safety Standards (HUD Code).

**New Appointments:**
- **Clayton Camblin** (User/Consumer) - Weatherization Field Specialist, Kansas Housing Resource Corporation
- **Brad DeHays** (General Interest) - Founder/CEO, Connect Housing Blocks
- **Steve Ervin** (General Interest) - SVP, Berkadia Commercial Mortgage
- **Todd Kopstein** (Producer/Retailer) - CEO, Cascade Financial
- **Andrew McCoy, Ph.D** (User/Consumer) - Director, Virginia Center for Housing Research, Virginia Tech
- **John Weldy** (Producer/Retailer) - VP Engineering, Clayton Homes Building Corp.

**Strategic Implication:** The appointment of John Weldy from Clayton Homes (the industry's largest manufacturer) alongside academic and housing research voices suggests HUD is balancing industry expertise with independent research perspectives. The focus statement from Federal Housing Commissioner Frank Cassidy emphasized "modernizing the HUD Code and reforming unnecessary barriers."

### "21st Century ROAD to Housing Act" Legislative Update
**Status:** Senate passed (89-10) on March 12, 2026  
**Source:** Manufactured Home Living News / WND Analysis

The Senate passed its version of housing legislation with strong bipartisan support, but the bill faces an uncertain path in the House. Key concerns raised:

- Critics note the bill doesn't directly address how to solve the housing shortage or increase new construction funding
- Some Republican lawmakers raising concerns over provisions prohibiting central bank digital currency
- Housing shortage estimates range from 4-8+ million units nationally

**Quote from HUD PD&R:** Pamela Blumenthal and Regina Gray noted that without closing the housing gap, "the housing crisis will not be solved, regardless of what a politician or special interest group may claim."
