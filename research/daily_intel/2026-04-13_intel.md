## 2. POLICY & REGULATORY NEWS

### HUD Updates

#### 1. White House Executive Orders on Housing (March 13, 2026)
**Two EOs signed by President Trump:**

**EO 1: "Removing Regulatory Barriers to Affordable Home Construction"**
- Terminates regulations that stall housing construction
- Cuts regulatory costs (~$94,000 per new single-family home)
- Targets "green" building codes adding $30,000+ to construction costs
- Overall 2025 deregulation projected to save Americans $212 billion

**EO 2: "Promoting Access to Mortgage Credit"**
- Reduces regulatory hurdles driving up borrowing costs
- Expands community bank participation in lending
- Aims to broaden mortgage access for first-time buyers, veterans, families

**HUD Response:**
- Secretary Scott Turner: "Homeownership is the bedrock of the American Dream, but that foundation crumbled under the Biden administration"
- HUD will terminate regulatory barriers, reform programs, incentivize state/local rule changes
- Will align Opportunity Zone incentives with single-family development
- Opportunity Zones have already created 400,000+ housing units in distressed communities
