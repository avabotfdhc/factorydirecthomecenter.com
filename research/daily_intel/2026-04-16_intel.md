**Source:** HUD.gov (Press Release HUD-No. 26-014, Feb 17, 2026)

**New Appointees:**

### Executive Orders on Housing Affordability & Mortgage Access
**Source:** HUD.gov (Press Release HUD-No. 26-022, March 13, 2026)  
**Impact:** HIGH

President Trump signed two Executive Orders:
1. **"Removing Regulatory Barriers to Affordable Home Construction"**
2. **"Promoting Access to Mortgage Credit"**

**Key Points:**
- Regulatory costs account for ~$94,000 of the final price of a new single-family home
- Green energy mandates can add $30,000+ to construction costs
- 2025 deregulation efforts projected to save Americans $212 billion collectively
- HUD will terminate regulatory barriers and incentivize states/localities to change burdensome rules
- Opportunity Zones aligned with single-family development and New Markets Tax Credit programs

**Manufactured Housing Specific:** HUD specifically mentions cutting "onerous energy and water requirements for manufactured homes" as part of the deregulation push.

**Strategic Note:** This signals a favorable regulatory environment for manufactured housing production with potential cost reductions.

### HUD Tribal Housing Investment
**Source:** HUD.gov (Press Release HUD-No. 26-024, April 10, 2026)  
**Impact:** MEDIUM

HUD announced $1.1 billion in Indian Housing Block Grant (IHBG) funding for Tribal communities. This marks the 30th anniversary of NAHASDA (Native American Housing Assistance and Self-Determination Act).

**Key Stats:**
- ~600 Tribal communities will receive funding
- NAHASDA has helped build/acquire/rehabilitate 160,000+ homes in Indian Country since 1996
- Supports housing development, modernization, and safety/crime prevention

**Strategic Note:** Potential opportunity for manufactured housing in Tribal community development projects.
