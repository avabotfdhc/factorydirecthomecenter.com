### Affordable HOMES Act Progress
**Source:** HousingWire

The U.S. House passed the "Affordable HOMES Act" (also referred to as "Housing for the 21st Century Act"), with the Senate responding with the "21st Century ROAD to Housing Act." The Senate version passed 89-10 on March 12, 2026.

**Key Provisions:**
- Removes regulatory burdens on manufactured housing development
- Gives HUD sole authority over manufactured housing energy efficiency standards
- Eliminates overlapping oversight by the Department of Energy
- Estimated potential price reduction: up to $10,000 per unit

**Critical Analysis:**
Industry observers note that while the legislation streamlines regulations, critics (including QZ and UnidosUS) point out it doesn't directly address the 4-8 million unit housing shortage. The bill focuses on preserving/building 1.3 million units over 10 years through a combination of production and preservation - far below the actual need.

## 2. Industry Market Intelligence

### Manufactured Housing as Affordable Alternative
**Source:** HousingWire, NAHB

- Manufactured homes sell for **less than one-third** the price of site-built homes
- **7.2 million U.S. households** currently live in manufactured homes
- Manufactured housing represents **5.4%** of all U.S. housing stock
- **55%** of manufactured homes are located in rural areas (vs. <25% of single-family houses)

**Top States by Manufactured Housing Share:**

**Appreciation Data:**

## 4. Industry Challenges

### Persistent Stigma & NIMBY Issues
**Source:** HousingWire, AscentDS

Gene Kim, Executive VP at AscentDS, notes:
> "When people say mobile home parks, a lot of times, their mind jumps to a 1960s trailer park. But the communities delivered today are of much higher quality. It's almost necessary to have a separate asset class and asset theme category."

**Common Misconceptions:**
- Manufactured communities reduce neighboring property values
- Negative impact on area aesthetics and crime rates
- Homes don't appreciate in value
- Higher infrastructure costs for municipalities

**Entitlement Challenges:**
Due to NIMBY activism, new manufactured communities tend to be larger (300+ sites) to justify the entitlement battle costs.

### Geographic Hotspots
