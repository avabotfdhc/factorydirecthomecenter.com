## What Are Manufactured Homes?

### The Official Definition

Manufactured homes are houses built entirely in a factory under the federal HUD Code (established in 1976), then transported to the home site in one or more sections. Unlike the "mobile homes" of the past, modern manufactured homes must meet strict federal standards for:

- **Structural design and construction**
- **Fire safety**
- **Energy efficiency**
- **Transportation durability**

### Manufactured vs. Modular vs. Mobile Homes

Understanding the terminology helps you make the right choice:

| Feature | Manufactured | Modular | Mobile (Pre-1976) |
|---------|--------------|---------|-------------------|
| **Building Code** | Federal HUD Code | State/local IRC | Pre-HUD (no code) |
| **Foundation** | Permanent chassis | Permanent foundation | Varies |
| **Financing** | Chattel or FHA | Conventional mortgage | Difficult |
| **Transport** | Wheels/chassis removable | Sections on flatbed | Wheels often permanent |
| **Appreciation** | Good on owned land | Similar to site-built | Poor |

**Bottom line:** Today's manufactured homes are not your grandparents' trailers. They're permanent housing solutions built to rigorous standards, with modern designs, energy efficiency, and financing options that make homeownership accessible.

## Financing Manufactured Homes in Indiana

### FHA Title I Loans

**Best for:** Buyers with limited down payment funds, homes not on permanent foundations

**Key features:**
- Down payment: As low as 5%
- Loan limits: Up to $92,892 (home only) or $138,762 (home + lot)
- Credit score: 580+ preferred
- Can finance home only or home + land

### FHA Title II Loans

**Best for:** Modular homes or manufactured homes on permanent foundations

**Key features:**
- Down payment: 3.5% minimum
- Similar rates to conventional mortgages
- Requires permanent foundation
- Longer terms available (up to 30 years)

### VA Loans

**Best for:** Veterans and active military

**Key features:**
- **0% down payment**
- Competitive interest rates
- No PMI required
- Can finance manufactured or modular homes
- Must meet VA and lender requirements

If you've served our country, VA financing is often your best option for manufactured home purchases.

### Conventional Mortgages

**Best for:** Modular homes, strong credit, larger down payments

**Key features:**
- Available for modular homes (IRC code)
- Some lenders offer for manufactured homes on permanent foundations
- Down payment: 5–20% typical
- Best rates for 740+ credit scores

### Chattel Loans

**Best for:** Homes in communities (leased land), faster closing

**Key features:**
- Treats home as personal property (like a car)
- Faster approval process
- Higher interest rates than mortgages
- Shorter terms (15–20 years typical)
- Lower closing costs

### Indiana-Specific Financing Considerations

**Property taxes:** Indiana's property tax caps (1% for homesteads) apply to manufactured homes on owned land, making ownership even more affordable.

**First-time buyer programs:** Indiana Housing & Community Development Authority (IHCDA) offers programs that may apply to manufactured home purchases on foundations.

**Credit unions:** Many Indiana credit unions offer competitive manufactured home financing with local decision-making.

## Common Myths About Manufactured Homes

### Myth: "Manufactured homes are poorly built"
**Reality:** Built to federal HUD Code with rigorous inspections. Many exceed site-built quality due to controlled factory conditions.

### Myth: "They don't appreciate in value"
**Reality:** On owned land with proper maintenance, manufactured homes can appreciate similarly to site-built homes. Location matters most.

### Myth: "They're not safe in storms"
**Reality:** Modern manufactured homes meet strict wind and structural standards. Proper anchoring is essential—professional installation ensures safety.

### Myth: "Financing is impossible"
**Reality:** Multiple financing options exist: FHA, VA, conventional, and chattel loans. Credit requirements vary by program.

### Myth: "They all look the same"
**Reality:** Extensive customization options let you personalize flooring, cabinets, countertops, exterior colors, and more.
