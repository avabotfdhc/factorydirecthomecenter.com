## Michigan Zoning and Placement

### On Owned Land

Michigan zoning for manufactured homes varies significantly by township and county:

**Generally permissive:**
- Agricultural zones
- Rural residential areas
- Unincorporated townships

**May be restricted:**
- Suburban subdivisions with covenants
- Some urban areas
- Historic districts

**Michigan-specific considerations:**
- **Wind zones:** Michigan has specific wind zone requirements for manufactured homes
- **Snow load:** Homes must meet Michigan snow load standards
- **Flood zones:** Special requirements in flood-prone areas

**We handle:** All permitting, zoning research, and compliance requirements as part of our service.

### In Manufactured Home Communities

Michigan has a significant number of manufactured home communities, though **29% are owned by private equity firms** (highest in the nation).

**Benefits of communities:**
- Amenities (pools, clubhouses)
- Maintenance included
- Social activities

**Considerations:**
- Monthly lot rent ($250-$600+)
- Community rules and restrictions
- Long-term lease terms
