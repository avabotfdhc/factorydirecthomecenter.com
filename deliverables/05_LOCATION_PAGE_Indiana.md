## Financing Manufactured Homes in Indiana

### Options Available

**FHA Title I Loans**
- Down payment: As low as 5%
- For homes on leased or owned land
- Credit score: 580+

**FHA Title II Loans**
- Down payment: 3.5%
- For permanent foundation homes
- Similar rates to conventional mortgages

**VA Loans**
- **0% down** for veterans
- Competitive rates
- No PMI required

**Conventional Mortgages**
- Available for modular homes
- Best rates for 740+ credit scores

**Chattel Loans**
- For homes in communities
- Faster approval
- Higher rates but lower closing costs

### Indiana-Specific Benefits

- **Property tax caps:** 1% for homesteads applies to manufactured homes on owned land
- **First-time buyer programs:** IHCDA programs may apply
- **Credit union financing:** Many Indiana credit unions offer competitive manufactured home loans

**Can I finance a manufactured home in Indiana?**
Yes! Options include FHA Title I and II loans, VA loans for veterans, conventional mortgages for modular homes, and chattel loans.

Typically 8-16 weeks for manufactured homes and 12-20 weeks for modular homes, depending on the model and factory schedule.

Most rural Indiana counties allow manufactured homes. Urban and suburban areas vary. We can help you research zoning for your specific property.
