=== REGULATORY & POLICY UPDATES ===

Major Policy Shift - Housing for the 21st Century Act:
- Removes permanent chassis requirement for manufactured homes
- Allows homes to be built without chassis while maintaining HUD Code compliance
- Reinforces HUD as primary regulator with uniform national standards
- Introduces small-dollar mortgage pilot program (under $100K) to expand financing access
- Streamlines environmental reviews and expands funding programs

HUD Code Updates (Effective Sept 2024):
- Most extensive updates in 30+ years (4th and 5th Sets)
- 74 updates to reference standards, 16 new standards, 3 regulatory text changes
- Clayton Homes and industry leaders have commended these updates

Local Zoning Progress:
- Encinitas, CA advancing zoning changes to treat manufactured homes same as single/multi-family homes
- Homes on permanent foundations qualify for residential zoning
- Post-June 1976 homes classified as manufactured homes (older = mobile homes)
