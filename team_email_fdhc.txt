**What Each of You Should Be Doing Every Single Day:**

1. **Own Your Follow-Up** — Speed wins. When a lead comes in, treat it like it's already yours to lose. Call within 5 minutes. Text within 10. Most sales go to the first person who actually responds.

2. **Know Your Product Cold** — Study the floor plans. Know the differences between manufactured, modular, and mobile. Understand financing options (chattel, FHA, VA, conventional). When a customer senses you know your stuff, trust follows.

3. **Qualify Early, Qualify Right** — Ask the questions that matter: budget range, timeline, land situation, credit picture. Don't waste time on unqualified leads, but don't write anyone off too early either. There's a loan program for almost everyone.

4. **Build Relationships, Not Transactions** — These are the biggest financial decisions of people's lives. Be patient with first-time buyers. Be confident with experienced ones. Meet them where they are and guide them forward.

5. **Track Everything** — Your CRM is your best friend. Log every call, every text, every objection. The data you collect today becomes the insight that closes deals tomorrow.

6. **Learn Something New Weekly** — Pick one thing: a new floor plan, a financing program, a competitor's weakness, a sales technique. Stack small improvements. In 6 months, you'll be unrecognizable.

**How to Get Better Every Day:**

- Review your lost deals. What could you have done differently? Be honest with yourself.
- Listen to your successful calls. What worked? Do more of that.
- Shadow a teammate who's crushing it. Ask questions. Steal what works.
- Read industry news. Know what's happening with interest rates, regulations, and market trends.
- Practice your pitch. Out loud. In the mirror. With a colleague. Confidence comes from repetition.
