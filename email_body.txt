Here is your step-by-step guide for recovering your locked LinkedIn account:

================================================================
STEP 1: SUBMIT LINKEDIN'S OFFICIAL RECOVERY FORM
================================================================

This is your first and most important step. LinkedIn will verify your identity and help you regain access.

Link: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/ask/TS-RHA

What to include in the form:
- Your full name
- The email address associated with your LinkedIn account
- Your LinkedIn profile URL (if you know it: linkedin.com/in/yourname)
- Brief description: I am locked out of my account and suspect unauthorized access

Expected response time: 24-72 hours

================================================================
STEP 2: SECURE YOUR EMAIL ACCOUNT
================================================================

Your email is the key to everything. Secure it immediately.

Actions to take:
1. Change your email password to a strong, unique passphrase
2. Check for unauthorized forwarding rules
3. Review recent login activity for suspicious access
4. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) if not already active
5. Check if any recovery phone numbers or backup emails were changed

================================================================
STEP 3: DOCUMENT EVERYTHING
================================================================

Keep records for your reference and for LinkedIn support:

- Screenshot any suspicious emails or notifications
- Note the date/time you last had access to LinkedIn
- List any changes you noticed before being locked out
- Save any emails from LinkedIn about account changes

================================================================
STEP 4: ADDITIONAL RESOURCES
================================================================

LinkedIn Help Center - Compromised Accounts:
https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1340402/reporting-a-hacked-account

LinkedIn Help Center - Two-Factor Authentication:
https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/531

================================================================
AFTER YOU REGAIN ACCESS
================================================================

Once LinkedIn restores your account:

1. Change your LinkedIn password immediately
2. Enable two-factor authentication on LinkedIn
3. Review active sessions and sign out of all devices
   Link: https://www.linkedin.com/settings/sessions
4. Check your email addresses and phone numbers on the account
5. Review recent posts, messages, and connection requests
6. Check for any new trusted devices you did not add
