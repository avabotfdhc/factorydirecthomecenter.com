=== 5. CUSTOMER REVIEWS & SENTIMENT ===

CRITICAL FINDING: Widespread dissatisfaction presents OPPORTUNITY for FDHC

CLAYTON HOMES - NEGATIVE SENTIMENT
- Net Promoter Score: -66 (82% Detractors)
- Top complaints: Poor craftsmanship, cheap materials, warranty issues, unresponsive service
- Recurring issues: Leaks, broken doors, unfinished interiors, soft/warping floors
- Service complaints: Runaround tactics, stalling until warranty expires, rude workers

CHAMPION HOMES - NEGATIVE SENTIMENT
- Similar pattern: Poor quality, shoddy workmanship, perpetual problems
- Top complaints: Water leaks, creaking floors, roof issues, mold (even in homes <1 year old)
- Service issues: Unreturned calls, refused repairs, it is your problem now attitude
- Legal action reported by some customers

SENTIMENT ANALYSIS INSIGHT
- Both major competitors suffer from quality control and post-sale service problems
- Customers feel abandoned after purchase
- Warranty process described as adversarial
- Quality perception: thinner than a child's swimming pool (Clayton), cheaply made (Champion)
