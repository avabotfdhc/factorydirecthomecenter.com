## Part 1: Website Audit — Critical Findings

### ❌ CRITICAL ISSUE #1: Missing Title Tags & Meta Descriptions
**What I Found:** Browser tabs show empty titles. No meta descriptions.
**Impact:** Google generates random snippets → terrible click-through rates
**Fix:** Write keyword-optimized titles for all 15 pages

### ❌ CRITICAL ISSUE #2: No Location Pages
**What I Found:** Zero state or city-specific landing pages
**Impact:** Can't rank for "manufactured homes Indiana" (high-volume searches)
**Fix:** Create 12 location pages targeting IN, MI, OH + major cities

### ❌ CRITICAL ISSUE #3: Thin Content
**What I Found:** Floor plans show only specs. No descriptions. No prices.
**Impact:** Google sees low-value pages → won't rank
**Fix:** Add 300-word descriptions, pricing, energy ratings to each model

### ❌ CRITICAL ISSUE #4: Blog is Dead
**What I Found:** Only 3 posts. Last "update" dated Oct 2025 (future date?)
**Impact:** Missing 50+ high-opportunity keyword targets
**Fix:** Publish 2 SEO-optimized posts per week

### ❌ CRITICAL ISSUE #5: No Lead Capture
**What I Found:** Email signup buried in footer. No exit popups. No sticky CTAs.
**Impact:** 95%+ of visitors leave without converting
**Fix:** Add exit-intent popup, sticky mobile CTAs, quote forms

### ❌ CRITICAL ISSUE #6: Image SEO Fails
**What I Found:** Every image has alt text: "FDHC Building" (useless)
**Impact:** Missing image search traffic + accessibility issues
**Fix:** Write descriptive alt text for all images

### ❌ CRITICAL ISSUE #7: Missing Schema Markup
**What I Found:** No structured data (LocalBusiness, Product, FAQ)
**Impact:** Missing rich snippets in search results
**Fix:** Add JSON-LD schema to all pages
