**Subject Line:** How an 8-year-old built a business

**Preview Text:** The story behind B's Wild And Wonder

---

**Email Body:**

```
Hi again!

I wanted to share a little more about how B's Wild And Wonder started.

A few months ago, I noticed something. My clothes kept falling apart. 
The stitching came loose, the pockets were too small for my treasures, 
and the designs were boring.

Then I noticed something else. Some kids in my community couldn't 
afford to go camping or hiking. They didn't have the gear, and their 
families couldn't pay for adventures.

I wanted to fix both problems.

So I built B's Wild And Wonder. Here's how I choose every product:

1. Find the problem ("These shorts don't have big enough pockets")
2. Design the fix ("What if we made deeper pockets?")
3. Test it ("I wore these for two weeks climbing trees")
4. Improve ("The pockets are good but the button broke")
5. Only then sell ("Now it's ready")

Every product in our store has been through my adventures. If it 
doesn't survive, it doesn't make the cut.

Plus, a portion of every sale helps fund outdoor adventures for 
kids in need. When you shop with us, you're helping another child 
discover the wonder of the outdoors.
```
