### The Braxton Method

**Title:** "How I Choose Every Product"

**Content:**  
"I'm not just the founder—I'm the Chief Adventure Tester. Here's how I decide what makes it into the store:

**Step 1: Find the Problem**  
I look for gear that doesn't work right. "These shorts don't have pockets big enough for my rocks."

**Step 2: Design the Fix**  
I figure out what would make it better. "What if we made deeper pockets with stronger stitching?"

**Step 3: Test It**  
I wear it, use it, and abuse it. "I wore these for two weeks climbing trees and rolling in grass."

**Step 4: Improve**  
If something breaks, I find something better. "The pockets are good but the button came loose. Let's try a snap."

**Step 5: Only Then Sell**  
"Now it's ready. I fixed all the problems."

Every product in this store has been through my adventures. If it doesn't survive, it doesn't make the cut."

## FAQ PAGE

### Page Title: Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q: Who is Braxton?**  
A: Braxton is the 8-year-old founder of B's Wild And Wonder. He personally tests every product in the store and makes sure it can survive real kid adventures. If it doesn't meet his standards, it doesn't get sold.

---

**Q: How did B's Wild And Wonder start?**  
A: Braxton noticed two problems: kids' clothes that fell apart too easily, and kids who couldn't afford outdoor adventures. He decided to build a business that solved both—selling quality adventure gear while giving back to help other kids explore.

---

**Q: What sizes do you carry?**  
A: Most of our clothing comes in sizes 2T through 12. Hats are typically one-size-fits-most with adjustable straps, or available in S/M/L. Plush toys and accessories list specific dimensions. Check individual product pages for exact sizing.

---

**Q: How do I find the right size for my child?**  
A: Each product page has a size chart with measurements. When in doubt, size up—kids grow fast! Our clothes are designed with a little extra room for active play. You can also check our Size Guide page for detailed measuring instructions.

---

**Q: What materials are your clothes made from?**  
A: We prioritize soft, durable materials like 100% cotton, cotton blends, and performance fabrics. Every product description lists the exact materials. Braxton tests everything to make sure it's comfortable and holds up to active play.

---

**Q: How long does shipping take?**  
A: Orders typically arrive within 7-14 business days. We work with partner warehouses to bring you the best selection at great prices. You'll receive a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

---

**Q: Do you ship to Canada?**  
A: Yes! We ship to both the US and Canada. Canadian orders may take slightly longer (10-18 business days) and may be subject to customs fees.

---

**Q: What is your return policy?**  
A: We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition. The customer is responsible for return shipping costs. See our full Return Policy for details.

---

**Q: How do I track my order?**  
A: Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can also log into your account on our website to check order status.

---

**Q: What if my item arrives damaged?**  
A: We're sorry if this happens! Please contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage, and we'll send a replacement or issue a full refund—whichever you prefer.

---

**Q: Do you offer gift wrapping?**  
A: Not currently, but we're working on it! In the meantime, our packaging is designed to be gift-ready with minimal extra wrapping needed.

---

**Q: Can I change or cancel my order?**  
A: We process orders quickly, but if you contact us within 2 hours of placing your order, we'll do our best to accommodate changes or cancellations. After that, the order may have already shipped.

---

**Q: How does the giving back program work?**  
A: A portion of every sale helps fund outdoor adventures for kids in need. We also run programs like "Buy a Hat, Give a Hat" where we donate items to children who need them. Braxton personally helps choose the organizations we support.

---

**Q: What charities do you support?**  
A: We support organizations that help kids access outdoor experiences. Braxton is involved in selecting these partners. Check our About page for current charity partners, or contact us to suggest an organization.

---

**Q: Are your products safe for babies?**  
A: Our products are designed for kids ages 0-12. Items for babies (0-24 months) are made with safe, soft materials. Always check product descriptions for age recommendations and safety information.

---

**Q: How do I wash and care for my items?**  
A: Care instructions are listed on each product page and on the garment tag. Most items are machine washable in cold water. When in doubt, wash cold and hang dry to preserve colors and shape.

---

**Q: Do you have a rewards program?**  
A: Not yet, but we're planning to launch one soon! Join our email list to be the first to know when our rewards program goes live.

---

**Q: How can I contact Braxton?**  
A: You can reach our team (including messages for Braxton!) at braxton@bwildandwonder.com. While Braxton is busy with school and testing products, he loves hearing from customers and tries to respond when he can!

---

**Q: Do you offer wholesale?**  
A: We're not currently offering wholesale, but we're open to partnerships. Contact us at wholesale@bwildandwonder.com to discuss opportunities.

---

**Q: Can I collaborate with B's Wild And Wonder?**  
A: We love working with like-minded brands, influencers, and organizations! Email us at partnerships@bwildandwonder.com with your proposal.

### How to Measure

**Chest:** Measure around the fullest part of the chest, keeping tape level  
**Waist:** Measure around the natural waistline  
**Height:** Measure from top of head to floor without shoes

---

| 4T | 4 years | 22-23" | 21-22" | 37-40" |

### Tips for the Best Fit

• **When in doubt, size up** — Kids grow fast and our clothes are designed with room to grow  
• **Check the product page** — Some items may have specific sizing notes  
• **Read reviews** — Other parents often mention if items run large or small  
• **Measure twice** — It only takes a minute and helps ensure the right fit
