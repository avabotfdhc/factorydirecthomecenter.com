## RETURN & REFUND POLICY

**Last Updated:** March 30, 2026

### Our Promise

We want you to love your B's Wild And Wonder gear! If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help.

### Return Eligibility

You may return items within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

To be eligible for a return:
- Item must be unused and unworn
- Item must be in original condition
- Item must have original tags attached
- Item must be in original packaging (if applicable)

### Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:
- Gift cards
- Items marked as final sale
- Items damaged through customer use
- Items without original tags

### Return Process

1. **Contact Us:** Email returns@bwildandwonder.com with your order number and reason for return
2. **Receive Authorization:** We'll send you a Return Authorization number and instructions
3. **Ship Item:** Pack the item securely and ship to the address provided
4. **Refund Processing:** Once received and inspected, we'll process your refund within 5-7 business days

### Return Shipping

- **Customer Responsibility:** Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the item was defective or incorrect
- **Original Shipping:** Original shipping costs are non-refundable
- **Free Returns:** We offer free return shipping on defective or incorrect items

### Refunds

Refunds will be issued to the original payment method. Please note:
- Credit card refunds may take 5-10 business days to appear on your statement
- PayPal refunds are typically processed within 24-48 hours
- Store credit is available immediately

### Exchanges

We offer exchanges for:
- Different sizes
- Different colors (if available)
- Defective items

To exchange an item, follow the return process above and specify that you'd like an exchange.

### Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item:
1. Contact us within 7 days of delivery
2. Include photos of the damage
3. We'll send a replacement or issue a full refund, including shipping costs

### Cancellations

You may cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it. After that, we begin processing and cannot guarantee cancellation.

### Questions?

Contact us at returns@bwildandwonder.com

## SHIPPING POLICY

**Last Updated:** March 30, 2026

### Shipping Destinations

We currently ship to:
- **United States** (all 50 states)
- **Canada**

We do not currently ship to other international destinations.

### Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days (Monday-Friday, excluding holidays).

During peak periods (holidays, sales), processing may take 2-3 business days.

### Shipping Methods & Timeframes

**United States:**
- Standard Shipping: 7-14 business days
- Express Shipping: 5-8 business days (additional fee)

**Canada:**
- Standard Shipping: 10-18 business days
- Express Shipping: 7-12 business days (additional fee)

Please note: These are estimates and not guarantees. Actual delivery times may vary.

### Shipping Rates

**United States:**
- Orders $50+: FREE Standard Shipping
- Orders under $50: $5.00 Standard Shipping
- Express Shipping: $12.00 (all orders)

**Canada:**
- Orders $75+: FREE Standard Shipping
- Orders under $75: $8.00 Standard Shipping
- Express Shipping: $18.00 (all orders)

### Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with:
- Tracking number
- Carrier information
- Link to track your package

You can also track your order by logging into your account on our website.

### Delivery Issues

**Lost Packages:**
If your tracking shows delivered but you haven't received your package:
1. Check with neighbors and household members
2. Contact your local post office
3. Email us at shipping@bwildandwonder.com

We'll work with you to locate your package or send a replacement.

**Damaged Packages:**
If your package arrives damaged:
1. Take photos of the damaged packaging and contents
2. Contact us within 48 hours
3. We'll file a claim and send a replacement

### Customs & Duties (Canada)

Canadian customers may be responsible for:
- Import duties
- Taxes
- Customs processing fees

These charges are determined by Canadian customs and are the responsibility of the customer.

### Address Changes

We can only change shipping addresses within 2 hours of order placement. After that, the order has been processed for shipment.

Please ensure your shipping address is correct before completing your order.

### Undeliverable Packages

If a package is returned to us as undeliverable due to:
- Incorrect address
- Refused delivery
- Unclaimed package

We'll contact you to arrange reshipment (additional shipping fees may apply) or issue a refund minus shipping costs.

### Holiday Shipping

During peak holiday periods, we recommend ordering early:
- Christmas: Order by December 10 for standard shipping
- Other holidays: Order 2 weeks in advance

### COVID-19 & Delays

Occasionally, shipping may be delayed due to circumstances beyond our control (weather, carrier delays, etc.). We'll keep you updated if there are any issues with your order.

### Questions?

Contact us at shipping@bwildandwonder.com

## COOKIE POLICY

**Last Updated:** March 30, 2026

### What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

### How We Use Cookies

**Essential Cookies:** Required for the website to function properly (shopping cart, checkout, account login)

**Analytics Cookies:** Help us understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it

**Marketing Cookies:** Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track their performance

**Preference Cookies:** Remember your settings and preferences for a better experience

### Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:
- **Chrome:** Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies
- **Safari:** Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
- **Firefox:** Preferences > Privacy & Security > Cookies
- **Edge:** Settings > Cookies and site permissions

Please note that disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

### Third-Party Cookies

We use services from third parties that may set cookies:
- Google Analytics
- Facebook Pixel
- Shopify
- Klaviyo

These third parties have their own privacy policies.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Please check back periodically for updates.
