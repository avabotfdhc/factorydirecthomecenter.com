## Day 8: Brand Voice & Messaging Framework

### 8.1 Brand Voice Guidelines

#### Voice Attributes:

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Warm** | Like a trusted friend, not corporate | "We're so glad you're here" vs "Welcome to our store" |
| **Encouraging** | Uplifting, empowering language | "You've got this, little explorer" |
| **Playful** | Fun without being childish | "Adventure awaits (and so does snack time)" |
| **Authentic** | Genuine, not salesy | "Made for real kids, real adventures" |
| **Inclusive** | Everyone belongs | "Let's do it together" |

#### Messaging Pillars:

1. **Adventure & Exploration**
   - Keywords: explore, discover, adventure, wonder, roam
   - Tone: Exciting but safe

2. **Togetherness & Community**
   - Keywords: together, we, us, family, friends
   - Tone: Connecting, belonging

3. **Growth & Possibility**
   - Keywords: grow, brave, dream, possible, best life
   - Tone: Encouraging, supportive

4. **Quality & Care**
   - Keywords: soft, cozy, safe, made with love
   - Tone: Reassuring, trustworthy

### 8.2 Copywriting Formulas

#### Product Description Formula (AIDA):
```
Attention: Hook with benefit or emotion
Interest: Describe features with benefits
Desire: Paint the picture of use
Action: Clear CTA with urgency or social proof
```

#### Homepage Formula (PAS):
```
Problem: Parent wants best for child
Agitation: Hard to find quality + meaning
Solution: B's Wild And Wonder products
```

#### Collection Page Formula:
```
Headline: Category + Benefit
Subhead: Emotional connection
Body: What makes these special
Filter/Shop CTA
```

### 13.2 FAQ Page

#### Heading:
```
Frequently Asked Questions
```

#### Shipping Questions:

**Q: How long will my order take to arrive?**
A: Orders typically ship within 1-3 business days. Delivery takes 5-10 
business days for US orders and 7-14 business days for Canadian orders. 
You'll receive a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

**Q: Do you offer free shipping?**
A: Yes! We offer free standard shipping on all US orders over $50 and 
Canadian orders over $75.

**Q: Can I expedite my shipping?**
A: Yes, express shipping is available at checkout for an additional fee. 
Express orders arrive in 3-5 business days (US) or 5-7 business days (Canada).

#### Returns & Exchanges:

**Q: What is your return policy?**
A: We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, 
unwashed, and have all tags attached. Please note that customers are 
responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is defective.

**Q: How do I start a return?**
A: Email us at hello@bwildandwonder.com with your order number and reason 
for return. We'll send you a return authorization and instructions.

**Q: Can I exchange for a different size?**
A: Yes! We offer free size exchanges within 30 days. Contact us and we'll 
help you get the perfect fit.

#### Product Questions:

**Q: What sizes do you offer?**
A: Our clothing ranges from newborn (0-6M) through youth size 12. Each 
product page includes a detailed size chart. When in doubt, size up—kids 
grow fast!

**Q: Are your products safe for babies?**
A: Absolutely. All our products meet or exceed CPSIA safety standards. 
We use non-toxic materials and test for lead, phthalates, and other 
harmful substances.

**Q: How do I care for my items?**
A: Care instructions vary by product and are listed on each product page 
and garment tag. Most items are machine washable in cold water. We 
recommend air drying or tumble drying on low to extend the life of your items.

#### Order Questions:

**Q: Can I modify or cancel my order?**
A: We process orders quickly, but if you contact us within 2 hours of 
placing your order, we'll do our best to accommodate changes.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and 
Shop Pay.

**Q: Do you offer gift wrapping?**
A: Yes! Select gift wrapping at checkout for $4.99. We'll include a 
personalized gift message and wrap your items in our signature adventure-themed paper.

```
**Height:** Stand child against a wall, mark the top of their head, and measure.
```
