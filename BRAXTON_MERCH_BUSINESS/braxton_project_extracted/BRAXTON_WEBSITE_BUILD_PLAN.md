## Project Overview

This master document serves as the central reference for building a complete, optimized Shopify e-commerce store for B's Wild And Wonder. The build follows a 6-stage methodology designed for maximum SEO impact, conversion optimization, and long-term profitability.

### Build Philosophy

1. **SEO-First Architecture** - Every page built with search engines and answer engines in mind
2. **Conversion-Optimized** - Product pages designed to sell, not just display
3. **Scalable Foundation** - Infrastructure that grows with the business
4. **Brand Cohesion** - Consistent visual identity across all touchpoints
5. **Data-Driven Decisions** - Product selection based on search volume and margin analysis

## Quick Reference: Brand Voice Guidelines

### Do:
- Use warm, encouraging language
- Include "we" and "together" to reinforce community
- Keep sentences simple and positive
- Address parents directly with empathy
- Celebrate childhood wonder and adventure

### Don't:
- Use overly corporate or stiff language
- Talk down to children or parents
- Use negative framing ("don't miss out" → "join the adventure")
- Overuse exclamation points
- Use generic e-commerce clichés

## Risk Mitigation

### Drop Shipping Risks
- **Quality Control:** Order samples before listing
- **Shipping Times:** Set clear expectations (10-20 days)
- **Stock Issues:** Monitor FashionTIY availability weekly
- **Returns:** Establish clear policy, factor into pricing

### SEO Risks
- **Competition:** Focus on long-tail keywords initially
- **Algorithm Changes:** Diversify traffic sources (email, social)
- **Indexation:** Submit sitemap, monitor Search Console

### Financial Risks
- **Cash Flow:** Start with minimal inventory investment
- **Ad Spend:** Begin with organic, add paid gradually
- **Chargebacks:** Use fraud protection, clear policies
