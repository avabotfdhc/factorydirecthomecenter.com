## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This plan builds a complete, SEO-optimized, conversion-focused Shopify store for Braxton's children's merchandise business. The strategy maximizes search visibility through AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), targets high-margin products with proven demand, and creates a brand story that converts.

**Key Differentiators:**
- 8-year-old founder story (media-worthy, authentic)
- Web3 structured data for AI search visibility
- 60 products across 4 categories (curated for search volume + margin)
- Complete SEO/AEO optimization for US/Canada
- Drop shipping model (no inventory risk)

- [ ] Enable lazy loading
- [ ] Minimize CSS/JS
- [ ] Use CDN (Shopify built-in)
- [ ] Target: <3 second load time

**Mobile Optimization:**
- [ ] Test all pages on mobile
- [ ] Touch-friendly buttons (min 44px)
- [ ] Readable font sizes (min 16px)
- [ ] No horizontal scrolling

**URL Structure:**
- [ ] Clean URLs: /products/adventure-graphic-tee
- [ ] No unnecessary parameters
- [ ] Canonical tags set
- [ ] 301 redirects for any changes

**Indexing:**
- [ ] XML sitemap submitted to Google
- [ ] Robots.txt configured
- [ ] Noindex on cart/checkout pages
- [ ] Schema markup validated

### AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

**Featured Snippet Targets:**

**Question:** What is the best clothing for adventurous kids?  
**Answer:** The best clothing for adventurous kids combines durability with comfort. Look for reinforced stitching, soft fabrics, and designs that encourage outdoor play. B's Wild And Wonder specializes in adventure-tested kids clothing built for tree-climbing, hiking, and exploring.

**Question:** How do I choose the right size for my child?  
**Answer:** Measure your child's chest, waist, and height, then compare to our size chart. When in doubt, size up—kids grow fast! Our clothes are designed with a little extra room for active play.

**Question:** What makes B's Wild And Wonder different?  
**Answer:** B's Wild And Wonder is built by 8-year-old Braxton, who personally tests every product. If it doesn't survive his adventures, it doesn't make the store. Plus, every purchase helps fund outdoor adventures for kids in need.

### Local SEO (US & Canada Focus)

**Geographic Signals:**
- Target keywords: "kids clothing USA," "children's gear Canada"
- Shipping pages mention US & Canada specifically
- Currency: USD primary, CAD secondary
- Shipping times by region
- Local business schema with US/CA addresses
