## Day 5: Product Selection Criteria

### 5.1 Selection Framework

#### Must-Have Criteria (All products must meet):
- [ ] Minimum 40% profit margin
- [ ] FashionTIY availability (in stock)
- [ ] Quality rating 4.5+ stars on FashionTIY
- [ ] Fits brand aesthetic (adventure, wonder, togetherness)
- [ ] Appropriate for ages 0-12
- [ ] Safe materials (BPA-free, non-toxic, CPSIA compliant)

#### Scoring Criteria (Rank 1-5):

| Factor | Weight | Measurement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Search Volume | 25% | Monthly keyword searches |
| Competition | 20% | Number of competing listings |
| Profit Margin | 25% | (Price - Cost) / Price |
| Trend Velocity | 15% | Search trend direction |
| Brand Fit | 15% | Alignment with B's Wild And Wonder |

### 5.2 Profit Margin Calculation

#### Formula:
```
Selling Price = FashionTIY Cost × 2.5 (minimum)
Shipping to Customer = $5.99 (US) / $9.99 (CA)
Transaction Fee = 2.9% + $0.30

Net Profit = Selling Price - FashionTIY Cost - Shipping - Transaction Fee
Margin % = (Net Profit / Selling Price) × 100
```

#### Example Calculation:
```
FashionTIY Cost: $8.00
Selling Price: $24.99
Shipping: $5.99 (customer pays, but factor into value)
Transaction Fee: $24.99 × 0.029 + $0.30 = $1.02

Net Profit: $24.99 - $8.00 - $1.02 = $15.97
Margin: ($15.97 / $24.99) × 100 = 63.9%
```

## Day 7: Supplier Integration & Inventory Planning

### 7.1 FashionTIY Integration

#### Account Setup:
- [ ] Create FashionTIY business account
- [ ] Verify business credentials
- [ ] Set up payment method
- [ ] Configure shipping preferences
- [ ] Download product catalog/API access

#### Product Sourcing Process:
1. Search FashionTIY using product names from catalog
2. Verify quality ratings (4.5+ stars required)
3. Check minimum order quantities (MOQ)
4. Request samples for top 10 products
5. Confirm shipping times to US/Canada
6. Document supplier SKUs for inventory tracking

### 7.2 Inventory Strategy

#### Drop Shipping Model:
- No physical inventory held
- Orders forwarded to FashionTIY
- FashionTIY ships directly to customer
- 10-20 day delivery window

#### Sample Inventory (Optional):
- Order samples of top 10 products
- Use for photography and quality verification
- Cost: ~$150-200
- Timeline: Order in Stage 1, receive in Stage 3

### 7.3 Quality Control Checklist

#### Product Verification:
- [ ] Material safety (CPSIA compliance)
- [ ] Size accuracy vs. description
- [ ] Color accuracy vs. photos
- [ ] Stitching quality (apparel)
- [ ] Durability testing (plush)
- [ ] Function testing (interactive items)

#### Packaging Standards:
- [ ] Branded packaging available?
- [ ] Gift wrapping options?
- [ ] Include thank you card/insert?
- [ ] Return address customization?
