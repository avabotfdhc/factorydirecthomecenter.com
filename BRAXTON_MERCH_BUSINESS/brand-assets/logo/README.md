## Logo Specifications

### Primary Logo (Circular Badge)
- **Center:** Bold blue "B"
- **Background:** Blue mountain silhouette
- **Accent:** Red heart with rays above B
- **Top text:** "HELPING OTHERS LIVE THEIR BEST LIFE"
- **Bottom text:** "WILD AND WONDER"
- **Border:** Double blue circle

### Colors
- **Primary Blue:** #4169E1 (Royal Blue)
- **Heart Red:** #E63946 (Bright Red)
- **White:** #FFFFFF
- **Dark BG Navy:** #1B3A5F (for white logo variant)

### Usage Guidelines

**DO:**
- Use the full logo for main branding
- Use the icon for small spaces (favicons, social avatars)
- Maintain clear space around the logo
- Use the white version on dark backgrounds only

**DON'T:**
- Stretch or distort the logo
- Change the colors
- Remove elements from the badge
- Use the dark logo on dark backgrounds
