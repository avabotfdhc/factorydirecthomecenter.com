## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### Image Sizes Reference
| Use Case | Width | Height | Aspect Ratio |
|----------|-------|--------|--------------|
| Homepage Hero | 1920px | 1080px | 16:9 |
| Category Banner | 1920px | 1080px | 16:9 |
| Product Main | 1200px | 1200px | 1:1 |
| Product Lifestyle | 1200px | 900px | 4:3 |
| Social Square | 1080px | 1080px | 1:1 |
| Social Story | 1080px | 1920px | 9:16 |
| Pinterest | 1000px | 1500px | 2:3 |
| Email Header | 1200px | 600px | 2:1 |
| Logo | 2000px | 2000px | 1:1 |
| Favicon | 512px | 512px | 1:1 |

### File Formats
- **Web:** WebP (primary), JPG (fallback)
- **Print:** PNG, TIFF
- **Logos:** SVG (source), PNG (web)
- **Favicon:** ICO, PNG

### Optimization Settings
- Quality: 85-90%
- Compression: Lossy for photos, lossless for logos
- Color Space: sRGB
- DPI: 72 for web, 300 for print
