🏠 DAILY INDUSTRY RESEARCH REPORT - APRIL 10, 2026

=== EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ===
The manufactured housing industry continues to face production challenges in 2026, with January shipments down 16.4% YoY. However, strong demand fundamentals persist driven by affordability pressures, with occupancy rates at 94.9% nationally. Key policy developments could reduce costs by up to 9% if passed.

=== 1. INDUSTRY NEWS & MARKET TRENDS ===

📉 PRODUCTION DECLINE CONTINUES
• January 2026: 7,417 new HUD Code homes produced (down 16.4% from 8,878 in Jan 2025)
• Industry has not fully recovered from Great Recession - shipments remain at just 1/3 of 1990s levels
• Top shipment states: Texas (1,254), Florida (566), North Carolina (452), Alabama (324), South Carolina (411)

📊 MARKET FUNDAMENTALS REMAIN STRONG
• Manufactured housing communities (MHCs) showing resilience as "defensive play"
• National occupancy: 94.9% (Pacific region near 99%)
• Asking rents: $752/month (up 7% YoY)
• Investment activity rebounded in 2025 with sales velocity up 66% YoY
• Cap rates compressed to 5.9%

🏗️ SEGMENT LEADERS
• Multi-section homes: 64.6% market share expected in 2026
• Wood construction: 38.9% market share
• North America: 46.3% of global market

=== 2. REGULATIONS & POLICY CHANGES ===

🔥 MAJOR DEVELOPMENT: Road to Housing Act
• Bipartisan bill passed unanimously by Senate committee (July 2025)
• Proposed change: Allow removal of manufactured home chassis after installation
• Impact: Could reduce home costs by up to 9% (~$10,000 on single-section homes)
• Only 5-7% of manufactured homes are ever moved - chassis mandate outdated since 1976
• Would enable placement in more communities (cities/suburbs), not just rural areas

📋 HUD CODE UPDATES (Effective Sept 2024)
• 4th and 5th Sets now in effect - most extensive updates in 30+ years
• 74 updates to reference standards, 16 new standards, 3 regulatory text changes
• Reinforces HUD as primary regulator, preserving uniform national standards

🏛️ TITLING REFORM PUSH
• Outdated state titling laws block buyers from accessing mortgages
• Homes titled as real estate qualify for mortgages (5.4% median rate)
• Homes titled as personal property use chattel loans (8.5% median rate)
• On $100K loan, mortgage borrowers save $25,000+ over loan life
• States like New Hampshire allowing home-only mortgages see savings up to $49,000

=== 4. CONSUMER DEMAND & BUYING PATTERNS ===

👥 DEMAND DRIVERS
• Housing affordability crisis pushing buyers toward manufactured housing
• First-time buyers and lower-income households seeking cost-effective options
• Faster construction speed and efficiency appealing to time-sensitive buyers
• Innovations in manufacturing/design elevating perceived quality

📈 BUYER SENTIMENT
• Manufactured housing described as "central tool in national affordability toolkit"
• Durable demographic demand + persistent affordability pressures + limited new supply = constructive outlook
• Lower all-in costs and faster delivery attracting cost-sensitive buyers

⚠️ BARRIERS
• Zoning and siting restrictions continue limiting new community development
• Infill and expansions becoming primary growth path
• Financing confusion (chattel vs mortgage) creates friction for buyers
