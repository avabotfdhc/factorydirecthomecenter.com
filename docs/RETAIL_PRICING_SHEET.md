## PRICING LEGEND

- **MSRP:** Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price
- **Factory Direct Savings:** Our discount off MSRP
- **Sale Price:** Base price after Factory Direct discount
- **Sale Price +1:** Price with 1 upgrade package
- **Sale Price +3:** Price with 3 upgrade packages
